What is a glimmer?

The term glimmer was introduced by therapist Deb Dana, based on polyvagal theory. Glimmers are small moments of safety that are easy to miss. Logging them helps you notice safety as readily as threat.

Questions and answers

How do I log from the Lock Screen?

Add the Glimmer and Trigger controls in Control Center or on the Lock Screen (iOS 18 or later), or add the Home Screen widget. A tap saves the time; add words later in the app.

What are the example entries?

The app shows a week of example entries on first launch so you can see how it works. Clear removes them.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Open the Pro sheet or Settings (gear) → Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

How do I move my entries to a new phone?

Settings → Backup → Export file, then Import file on the new phone.
