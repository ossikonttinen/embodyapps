Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Everything stays on your phone, and there are no ads or tracking.

What does Pro cost?

7,99 € a year with a 7-day free trial, or 17,99 € once for life.

How do I cancel the subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Affirmation of Life.

English, Finnish, German, Spanish, French, Japanese and Portuguese (Brazil).
