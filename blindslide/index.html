Play with your senses.

Blindslide is a collection of four reaction games played by sound, vibration and touch. You can play it entirely without seeing the screen.

Coming to the App Store Get notified at launch →
How it works

Every part has its own sound and place

  • The screen is split into 2, 4, 6 and finally 8 parts
  • Each part has its own pitch and direction, and its number
  • Before the game, the parts are introduced one by one, and speech describes the layout
  • You do not need to hit the coloured piece: a tap counts for the area it is in
Games

Four games, five levels

CountThe phone pulses 1–8 times. Count the pulses and tap the section with that number.
RepeatA series of sounds comes from one section. Tap that section as many times.
MelodyNeighbouring sections light up as a melody. Repeat the same route.
RecallSections flash and sound in a sequence. Repeat it in the same order.
Answering

Tap, knock or tilt

  • Tap the part on the screen, or slide your finger across the parts
  • Knock mode: answer by knocking the phone – even in your pocket
  • Tilt mode: tilt the phone towards the part like a spirit level
  • Mistakes shrink the pieces away from the edges; eight in a row takes one mistake back
Built for touch

No need to look

Gestures instead of buttons: hold a game to start, hold during a game to pause, swipe up and down to change level, right for instructions and left for settings. A short practice before each game the first time. Speech in Finnish or English at three speeds; sounds, vibration, speech and texts can each be turned on or off.

Support

Questions and answers

Can I really play without seeing?

Yes. Everything is told by sound, speech and vibration, and the tutorial teaches the gestures. If you play blind, I would love to hear how it works for you.

How do I start a game?

Hold the game in the menu. Hold again during the game to pause; holding while paused returns to the menu.

Does it work with VoiceOver?

Yes. The game area takes touches directly, so you can keep VoiceOver on: gestures go straight to the game, and Blindslide speaks for itself.

Which languages?

Finnish and English, including speech. Switch in the settings.

I bought all levels on another device.

Settings → Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Contact: hello@embodyapps.fi. Tell me your iPhone model and iOS version.

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Blindslide collects nothing. Settings and high scores stay on your own phone.
